Estia

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Estia is a Mac writing app made by Andy Nicolaides. In this policy, “I” and “me” refer to Andy as Estia’s developer. This page explains what information I collect through Estia, and which network connections Estia makes on your behalf when you use features that need them.

In short

  • You don’t need an Estia account.
  • Your writing is stored as files on your Mac, in a location you choose.
  • Estia has no developer-operated analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • I don’t receive your writing through Estia’s normal operation.
  • Estia does connect to other services when you ask it to: the feeds and websites you add, and a publishing service if you choose to publish.

What I collect

Through Estia’s normal operation, I don’t collect information about you or your writing. Specifically, Estia:

  • doesn’t require an Estia account;
  • doesn’t contain analytics operated by me;
  • doesn’t contain advertising;
  • doesn’t track you;
  • doesn’t send your writing to any server operated by me or for Estia;
  • doesn’t provide a cloud storage service for your writing.

If you email me for support, I’ll receive your message, your email address and anything you choose to include, as with any email.

Your writing

Estia stores your writing locally on your Mac as files, in a location you choose. Estia doesn’t upload it to me or to any Estia service. If the location you choose is synced by another service, that service’s own terms and privacy practices apply.

Connections Estia makes for you

Some features need to reach the internet. When they do, Estia communicates directly with services you’ve selected or asked it to use. These are not services I operate, and I don’t receive this information.

Feeds and sources

Estia makes network requests directly to the RSS and Atom feeds and other sources you configure. When you open a source, Estia may fetch the article or page content directly from the website it came from. As with visiting any website, those sites receive the request from your Mac, including information such as your IP address.

Writing assistance

Estia’s Get Started and Suggest Outline features use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models, on Macs that support them. They only run when you explicitly choose to use them. Estia doesn’t send your writing to any AI server operated by me or for Estia. Apple’s own features are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Publishing

Estia can optionally publish to a service you’ve set up, such as Pika or another service that supports Micropub. Estia only communicates with that service when you explicitly choose to publish, and it sends what’s needed to publish your post to the service you configured.

Credentials

Where an optional service needs you to sign in or provide a token, Estia stores those credentials using macOS system facilities, such as the Keychain, where applicable.

Third-party services

Feed publishers, websites, publishing services and Apple each have their own privacy practices and policies. I don’t operate those services and can’t speak for how they handle information, so please review their policies if you have questions about them.

Changes to this policy

If Estia’s behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy and Estia, email apps@thedent.net.
