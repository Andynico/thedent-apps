Estia

A quiet place to find inspiration and write.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

About Estia

Estia is a native Mac app for writers who start from what they read. It gathers the sources you follow, helps you notice something worth writing about, and then gives you a calm, focused place to write it.

Start from what you read

Estia brings your RSS and Atom sources together as a pool of potential inspiration rather than an inbox to clear. It is deliberately a writing app, not a full RSS reader: the sources are there to help you find something worth writing about.

A focused place to write

A native macOS writing environment built around Markdown. Write a piece that grows out of a source, or a link post that points to something you’ve found.

Plain files, on your Mac

Your writing is saved locally as ordinary Markdown files, in a folder you choose. There’s no Estia account, and your writing isn’t uploaded to Estia servers.

A little help getting started

On supported Macs, Estia can suggest a broad outline for a piece using Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. It only runs when you ask, and it offers a framework to write within, not a finished article. The writing is still yours.

Availability

Estia is coming soon to the Mac App Store.
