About Estia

When it comes to writing, we all need a little inspiration, and a quiet space to let that inspiration become something more. This is why I created Estia. A quiet space for writing, with thoughts and insights from your favourite writers close at hand when you need them.

Start from what you read

Add your favourite writers, blogs and websites to Estia and their latest posts are always close at hand. There’s no inbox to clear or endless list of articles to work through. They’re simply there when you’re looking for a little inspiration.

A focused place to write

When you find that spark of inspiration, Estia gives you a quiet place to get your thoughts onto the page. Write without distraction, create a quick link post when something is worth sharing, or just start writing from scratch, and when you’re ready, publish directly to your blog or save the files locally to upload later.

Plain files, on your Mac

Your writing belongs to you. Estia saves everything locally as ordinary Markdown files, in a folder you choose. There’s no Estia account and nothing is uploaded to Estia servers. Your files are always there for you to open, edit, move or back up however you like.

A little help getting started

Sometimes getting started is the hardest part. When you need a little help, Estia can use Apple Intelligence to suggest a simple structure for your writing based on what inspired you. It only happens when you ask, and it won’t write the article for you. The words, thoughts and ideas are still yours.

Availability

Estia is coming soon to the Mac App Store. It requires macOS 26 or later.
